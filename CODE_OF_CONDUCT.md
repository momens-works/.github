# Code of Conduct

Momens projects should be calm, respectful, and useful places to work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be specific, kind, and direct.
- Assume good intent while still naming risks clearly.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy, credentials, customer data, and unreleased product details.
- Make room for different disciplines: product, design, engineering, operations, and support.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information, credentials, or security issues in public.
- Repeatedly derailing technical or product discussions.
- Deliberately disrupting repositories, issues, reviews, or communication channels.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, decline contributions, or restrict participation when behavior conflicts with these expectations.
